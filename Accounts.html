How Financial Auto-Reconciliation Operates

1. Physical Statement Balance: Entered by the Treasurer directly from the official bank or M-Pesa statement along with the verification date.

2. System Book Balance: Automatically summed from approved cash inflows (contributions, repayments) minus outflows (loan disbursements, expenses).

3. Expense Offsets / Refunds: Handled seamlessly and excluded from physical bank inflows to keep bank reconciliations accurate.
