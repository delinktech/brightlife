Chama Financial Reconciliation Principles

1. System Inflows: Dynamically computed from all member contributions and loan repayments with status Approved mapped to each account.

2. Physical Bank Statement: The Treasurer updates the statement balance and date when downloading weekly/monthly e-statements from Equity Bank, CIC Group (MMF), or Mansa X.

3. Expense Offsets / Refunds: When a member pays for a group project out-of-pocket and receives offset credit on savings or loans, the transaction is categorized as Refund / Expense Offset. It is tracked separately and excluded from physical bank inflows to keep bank reconciliations accurate.
